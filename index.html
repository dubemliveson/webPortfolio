Our Story

Founded in 2015 by Chef Chidubem Nkemka, Chidubem Eats brings the heart and soul of Nigerian cuisine to your plate. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into a culinary destination celebrated for its authentic flavors and warm hospitality.

Our recipes have been passed down through generations, preserving the authentic tastes of Nigeria while incorporating contemporary culinary techniques. We source only the freshest ingredients, many imported directly from Nigeria, to ensure that each dish retains its traditional flavor profile.
